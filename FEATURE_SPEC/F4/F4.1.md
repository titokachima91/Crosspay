## Scope

Give users their transaction history from server data: a filterable, searchable ledger plus export and analytics.

**Key components (plan)**
- Transaction history with filters
- Search by trade reference or recipient
- Status-change timeline
- Export (CSV minimum)
- Spending analytics aggregated by corridor / currency

**Product rule (enforced)**: no mocks, nothing hardcoded in `lib/` - the history screen shows a plain "not connected" state until the backend ships the history endpoints.
